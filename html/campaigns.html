🌍 VoteClimate Election Campaigns

Made climate action unavoidable in Canadian politics: blanketing cities, suburbs, and digital spaces with impossible-to-miss voter campaigns. From transit ads to targeted mailers, our non-partisan messaging armed citizens with climate report cards, voting records, and clear demands, turning quiet concern into an electoral roar that politicians couldn't ignore.

👥 Candidate Support and Election Campaigns

We have worked with a wide range of political candidates across the spectrum. The common theme is supporting good candidates with a commitment to climate solutions and humanitarianism. Our role generally is strategic communications and operations support and the management of teams to implement, iterate and optimize campaign strategies and tactics.

🌲 Tanker Free BC Campaign

We were founding members of the first affinity group which came together to draw attention to and attempt to stop the Kinder Morgan pipeline. This became the roots of successful resistance, delaying construction for over a decade and preventing millions of tonnes of annual carbon emissions.

⛏️ Raven Coal Mine Victory

Coordinated a campaign that ultimately halted the proposed Raven Coal Mine, protecting critical coastal forest ecosystems and preventing irreversible damage to sensitive watersheds and wildlife habitats on Vancouver Island.

🧪 Thor Chemicals Plant Shutdown (South Africa)

Our investigation into mercury poisoning near the Thor Chemical plant mobilized health studies, UN intervention, and ultimately forced closure—protecting thousands of workers and residents.

♿ Disability Without Poverty

Helped the leaders of the national fight for economic justice for Canadians with disabilities by mobilizing stakeholders for engagements that shaped policy debates, and helped build unprecedented public support for equitable solutions.
