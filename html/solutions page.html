OUR SOLUTIONS

We create replicable solutions that turn fights into futures.

🏃‍♀️ The Great Climate Race Platform

Canada’s most inventive climate fundraiser - mobilizing 15,000+ runners and donors to directly fund solar and wind projects through a first-of-its-kind peer-to-peer platform. The real finish line? Proving that climate action could be joyful, viral, and relentlessly practical

🌱 Cool Neighborhoods Program

Reimagined climate action at the hyper-local level; turning 27 BC neighborhoods into living labs for low-carbon living, community resilience, and radical neighbor-to-neighbor change. Subject of Royal Roads white paper, awards from City of West Vancouver, United Nations

📊 Carbon Budget Project/eDemocracy

As part programmers, part community organizers, part policy whisperers, we
turned data into drama: making dry numbers feel urgent
made officials crave citizen input; not just tolerate it
helped neighbors argue solutions, not just protest

By translating IPCC science into actionable municipal dashboards, we put carbon accountability directly in the hands of voters and councillors alike.

🌍 Climate Coordination Network

climate funding from the blockchain up: a community-powered process with quadratic algorithmic match funding that has been transparent, democratic, and ruthlessly focused on impact. Funding 471 small climate projects over 3 years in 156 countries around the world.

🍻 CRED – Conversations for Responsible Economic Development

A business-led initiative designed to shift corporate climate engagement from resistance to leadership by reframing environmental action as economic opportunity. CRED mobilized hundreds of businesses to advocate for stronger climate policies, proving that sustainability and profitability could align. Through research, reports, executive roundtables, and media partnerships, it helped corporate leaders see climate action as a competitive and economic advantage.
